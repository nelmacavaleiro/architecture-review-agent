# ADR-0004 Use GOV.UK Pay for online card payments

Status: Accepted
Date: 2024-03-11
Context: Several online forms took card payments through different payment pages, which made reconciliation slow.
Decision: Online card payments from residents and businesses use GOV.UK Pay.
Consequences: Payment card details are not handled by council systems. Finance must reconcile GOV.UK Pay reports with the finance system.
