## WBC-P05 Protect residents' personal data

Type: Mandatory
Statement: Residents' personal data is stored in the UK, and a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) is completed before any new processing that is likely to be high risk.
Rationale: The council must comply with UK GDPR, and handles sensitive information about housing, benefits and vulnerable residents.
Implications: Proposals must say what personal data is used, where it is stored, who can access it and how long it is kept, and whether a DPIA has been started.
Exceptions: Only with written approval from the Data Protection Officer.
