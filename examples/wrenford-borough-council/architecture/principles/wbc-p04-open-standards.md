## WBC-P04 Use open standards and avoid lock-in

Type: Guidance
Statement: Systems exchange data using open standards, and every new supplier contract includes a way to get the council's data out in a usable format.
Rationale: Follows the Government's Open Standards principles, and protects the council's ability to change supplier at the end of a contract.
Implications: Proposals should name the data formats and interfaces used, and describe how data would be exported if the council changed supplier.
Exceptions: Discussed at the Digital Design Authority.
