Pack: examples/wrenford-borough-council

Expected overall risk: High
Expected recommendation: Revise and resubmit
Expected status: Ready for architect review

## Expected findings

| # | Reference | Expected result | Expected severity | Where in the proposal | Acceptable alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | WBC-P05 Protect residents' personal data (storage in the UK) | Breach | High | Proposed design: conversation data in the supplier's US data centres | None |
| 2 | WBC-P05 Protect residents' personal data (DPIA) | Breach or concern | High | Data: DPIA judged not needed, although conversations may include housing and personal circumstances | Medium, as a concern; may be combined with finding 1 if both points are raised |
| 3 | WBC-P01 Start with user needs and keep an assisted route | Breach | Medium | Non-functional needs: general enquiries phone line will close | None |

## Traps (should not be reported as problems)

- Accessibility: a WCAG 2.2 AA report and council testing are described, so WBC-P02 and NFR-04 are met.
- Penetration testing: the supplier's yearly independent test meets NFR-06.
- Whole-life cost: set-up, running costs and contract length are given, so WBC-P08 is met.

## Notes

A good review may raise the risk of wrong answers from the language model, passing personal data to staff by email, or the lack of an exit plan (WBC-P04). These are acceptable as Low observations or questions. A reviewer may also cite ADR-0001 (AWS in the London region) alongside finding 1; this is acceptable, although WBC-P03 allows software-as-a-service products.
