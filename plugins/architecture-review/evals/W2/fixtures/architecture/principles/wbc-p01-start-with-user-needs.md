## WBC-P01 Start with user needs and keep an assisted route

Type: Guidance
Statement: Services are designed around the needs of the residents and staff who use them, and anyone who cannot use the online service can get help to complete the task another way.
Rationale: Follows the first point of the Government's Technology Code of Practice, and recognises that some residents cannot or prefer not to use online services.
Implications: Proposals should describe who the users are, how their needs were found, and how residents without online access will be supported, for example by telephone or at the customer service centre.
Exceptions: Discussed at the Digital Design Authority.
