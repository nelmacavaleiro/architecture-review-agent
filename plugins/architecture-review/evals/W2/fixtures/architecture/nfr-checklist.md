# Wrenford Borough Council non-functional requirements

## NFR-01 Availability

Applies to: Online services for residents
Target: Available 99.5% of the time each month, with planned maintenance outside 7am to 10pm.

## NFR-02 Recovery

Applies to: Services that take payments or record housing and council tax cases
Target: Service restored within 8 hours; no more than 1 hour of data lost.

## NFR-03 Recovery for other services

Applies to: All other services
Target: Service restored within 2 working days; no more than 24 hours of data lost.

## NFR-04 Accessibility

Applies to: Websites, online forms and apps for residents or staff
Target: Meets WCAG 2.2 level AA, tested before go-live and after major changes.

## NFR-05 Performance

Applies to: Online services for residents
Target: Pages load within 3 seconds on a typical mobile connection.

## NFR-06 Security testing

Applies to: New internet-facing services
Target: Independent penetration test before go-live, and every year after that.
