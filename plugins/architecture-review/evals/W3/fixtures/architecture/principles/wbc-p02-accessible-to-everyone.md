## WBC-P02 Accessible to everyone

Type: Mandatory
Statement: Websites, online forms and apps for residents or staff meet the public sector accessibility regulations, tested against WCAG 2.2 level AA.
Rationale: The council has a legal duty under the public sector accessibility regulations, and many residents rely on assistive technology.
Implications: Proposals must describe accessibility testing before go-live, and suppliers must provide evidence that their products meet the standard. An accessibility statement must be published for each service.
Exceptions: Only where the regulations allow, with a documented assessment approved by the Head of Digital.
