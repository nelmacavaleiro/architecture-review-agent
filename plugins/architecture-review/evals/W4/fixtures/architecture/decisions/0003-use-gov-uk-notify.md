# ADR-0003 Use GOV.UK Notify for emails, text messages and letters to residents

Status: Accepted
Date: 2024-01-22
Context: Each service sent messages to residents in its own way, with different templates and suppliers.
Decision: Services send emails, text messages and letters to residents through GOV.UK Notify.
Consequences: Consistent messages and less supplier management. Services must design their messages as Notify templates.
