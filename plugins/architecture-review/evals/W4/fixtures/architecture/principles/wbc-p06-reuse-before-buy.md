## WBC-P06 Reuse before buying, and buy before building

Type: Guidance
Statement: Before buying or building something new, teams check whether the council, another council or a shared government service already provides it.
Rationale: Follows the Technology Code of Practice on sharing and reuse, and saves public money.
Implications: Proposals should show which existing services were considered, including GOV.UK Notify and GOV.UK Pay (see ADR-0003 and ADR-0004), and why they were or were not chosen.
Exceptions: Discussed at the Digital Design Authority.
