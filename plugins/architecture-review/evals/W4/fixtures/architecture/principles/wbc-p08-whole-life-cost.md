## WBC-P08 Show the whole-life cost

Type: Guidance
Statement: Proposals show the full cost over the life of the service, not only the cost to build or buy it.
Rationale: The council's budget is fixed, and running costs such as licences, hosting and support continue for years after a project ends.
Implications: Proposals should include set-up costs, yearly running costs, the expected contract length, and the cost of leaving at the end.
Exceptions: Discussed with the Finance business partner.
