# ADR-0008 Move the council website to LocalGov Drupal

Status: Proposed
Date: 2026-09-28
Context: The council website runs on a proprietary content management system whose licence is due for renewal in 2027.
Decision: Move the website to LocalGov Drupal, an open-source platform developed and shared by UK councils.
Consequences: Reuses work shared by other councils (WBC-P06) and avoids licence costs. The council would need a hosting and support arrangement, and staff training.
