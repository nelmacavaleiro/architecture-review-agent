## WBC-P07 Each type of case data has one system of record

Type: Mandatory
Statement: Each type of case data, such as housing applications or council tax accounts, is held and updated in one system of record. Other systems read it through agreed integrations and do not keep their own editable copies.
Rationale: Duplicate copies of case data lead to errors, conflicting decisions and difficult subject access requests.
Implications: Proposals must name the system of record for each type of case data they use, and describe how they read or update it (see ADR-0006).
Exceptions: Only with written approval from the Head of Digital and the relevant service director.
